PROJECT SUGAM — FINAL QR FLOW (NO WEBSITE SONG REVEAL)

1. Generate and print the WELCOME QR (card 0) for your handmade birthday card. It opens the home page.
2. Sugam selects ACCEPT THE MISSION. The site sends him to the bathroom mirror for his first hidden QR.
3. Mirror QR 1 -> bookshelf riddle -> bookshelf QR 2.
4. Bookshelf QR 2 -> shoe rack riddle -> shoe rack QR 3.
5. Shoe rack QR 3 -> confetti and wrapped diecast car -> refrigerator riddle -> refrigerator QR 4.
6. Refrigerator QR 4 -> wardrobe riddle -> wardrobe QR 5.
7. Wardrobe QR 5 -> confetti and wrapped Fossil watch -> sofa riddle -> sofa QR 6.
8. Sofa QR 6 -> electronics riddle -> electronics QR 7.
9. Electronics QR 7 -> confetti and wrapped RC car -> dining table riddle -> dining QR 8.
10. Dining table QR 8 -> FINAL bedside-drawer riddle. When he answers correctly, the website celebrates, says CASE CLOSED, and directs him to the bedside drawer. The mystery ends here.
11. Put the framed song QR surprise at the bedside drawer. There is NO website QR card or website song link at the bedside drawer. He discovers the frame physically.

QR PRINTING: Open YOUR-PUBLISHED-SITE/qr-cards.html and paste the published site's base address, including repository path if any. Generate the 9 QR codes total: one welcome QR plus hidden QR cards 1–8. Reprint cards if using an older version with a ninth hidden QR.

PUBLISHING: Replace index.html and qr-cards.html on GitHub Pages and commit to main. Allow deployment to finish, then test the full route.
