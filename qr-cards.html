Project SUGAM — QR Cards

After publishing the website, paste its base address below. Example: https://your-site.example (no ?stage= at the end). The cards below will generate QR codes for the nine hiding spots. Print this page, cut out the cards, and place each one at the matching location.

QR cards are intentionally labeled with neutral titles so Sugam won't know what's waiting for him. Don't leave this printer page open for him to see.
